# 📘 Course Syllabus: ECO 231 — Principles of Macroeconomics

## Course Overview

This course introduces macroeconomics and the study of national and global economic systems. Students examine how economies grow, how employment and inflation are measured, and how governments and institutions influence economic performance.

**Real-world application:** macroeconomics helps explain business cycles, inflation, labor markets, government policy, and economic decision-making in everyday life and career settings.

### Learning Outcomes

- Explain core macroeconomic concepts such as GDP, inflation, and unemployment.
- Analyze how economic activity affects households, businesses, and governments.
- Describe the role of fiscal and monetary policy in economic stability.
- Evaluate how national and global economic conditions shape decisions and opportunities.
- Interpret basic economic indicators and trends.

### Topics Covered

- Fundamentals of macroeconomics
- Gross domestic product and economic output
- Inflation and unemployment
- Fiscal policy and government influence
- Monetary policy and banking systems
- Business cycles and economic growth
- International trade and global economics
