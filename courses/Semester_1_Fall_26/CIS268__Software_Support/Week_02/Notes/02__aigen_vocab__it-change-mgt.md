# Vocabulary Sidebar: IT Change Management

- **IT Service Management (ITSM):** the broad discipline of delivering, supporting, controlling, and improving IT services; ex: the overall people, processes, policies, and tools used to support company email, laptops, networks, and business applications.
- **ITSM platform:** software used to operate ITSM workflows and records; ex: BMC Remedy/Helix, ServiceNow, and Jira Service Management can handle tickets, requests, changes, approvals, and configuration data.[^1][^2]
- **Change management (IT):** a controlled process for assessing, authorizing, scheduling, implementing, documenting, and reviewing changes to IT services; ex: approving a production security patch only after checking risk and affected applications.
- **Change enablement:** ITIL 4’s preferred term for IT change management; ex: ensuring a needed software upgrade can happen with the least practical risk and service disruption.[^3][^4]
- **Configuration management:** the discipline of tracking IT components, their settings or versions, and their relationships; ex: documenting that a web application depends on a database, an identity service, and certain servers.[^5][^6]
- **Configuration Management Database (CMDB):** a repository that records configuration items and their dependencies; ex: using the CMDB before a server patch to identify every application that might be affected.[^7][^5]
- **Configuration item (CI):** any IT component tracked because it supports a service; ex: a server, application, database, router, virtual machine, software version, or cloud resource.
- **Incident:** an unplanned service interruption or reduction in service quality; ex: users cannot connect to the VPN or access the payroll application.
- **Electronic trouble-ticket system:** software that records, assigns, tracks, escalates, and documents IT support work; ex: a user submits “My VPN is down,” and the ticket is routed to the network team for resolution.
- **BMC Remedy / BMC Helix ITSM:** Remedy is the longstanding enterprise ITSM and ticketing platform familiar in many organizations in the 2000s; Helix ITSM is BMC’s newer, cloud-oriented successor family with modernization, automation, self-service, integrations, and AI-related capabilities.[^2][^8][^9]
