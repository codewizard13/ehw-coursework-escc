# 📘 Course Syllabus: CIS 268 — Software Support

## Course Information

- **Credits:** 3 credit/semester hours
- **Delivery:** Fully online
- **Location:** Online
- **Dates:** August 19 – December 9, 2026
- **Prerequisites:** None

This course provides hands-on practical experience installing computer software and operating systems and troubleshooting common problems. It helps prepare students for the CompTIA A+ certification. The course is a suitable substitute for CIS 239, Networking Software, and is a CORE course for the AAT and AAS CIS programs when used in those programs.

## Course Objectives

By the end of this course, students should be able to:

1. Explain the roles and responsibilities of a technical support professional, including the purpose and use of ticketing systems.
2. Describe and compare core features of major operating systems, including Windows, Linux, and macOS.
3. Install, configure, and update operating systems to meet user and organizational requirements.
4. Explain virtualization concepts and create and manage virtual machines.
5. Apply system management tools to monitor, configure, and maintain computer systems.
6. Implement and support remote access solutions for system administration and end-user assistance.
7. Manage users, groups, updates, backups, recovery processes, and common boot or startup issues.
8. Apply file-system and storage-management concepts across multiple operating systems.
9. Explain and configure basic networking concepts and connectivity for computing devices.
10. Apply security and device-management best practices, including access control, power management, and data protection.

## Policies and Important Requirements

### Attendance Verification

The mandatory attendance verification quiz documents attendance. Students who do not complete it will be reported to the Registrar as a no-show and administratively removed from the course. It must be completed before Pell Grant and student-loan disbursement.

### Assignments and Late Work

Assignments are due on the dates listed in Canvas modules, the Canvas Calendar, the course schedule, and this syllabus. Work submitted after the due date receives a zero. Extenuating circumstances may be considered when the instructor is notified at least 24 hours before the due date or within 24 hours after it; documentation may be requested.

### Proctoring

The course includes an Honorlock practice quiz and one proctored quiz available in Canvas Modules. Honorlock requires a Chrome extension, valid photo ID, and a scan of the testing environment. Audio, video, and the computer screen are recorded and reviewed by instructors. Students are responsible for arranging and completing proctored assessments.

### Withdrawal

The last day to withdraw without academic penalty is **December 1, 2026**. Withdrawal may affect federal financial-aid eligibility.

### Academic Honesty and AI

All submitted work must be the student's own and written exclusively for this course. Reusing work from another course is academic dishonesty. Plagiarism may result in a zero, a final course grade of F, and referral to the Dean of Instruction. Students may use AI to revise and improve their own writing, but submitting work that is not their own violates the Academic Honesty policy.

### Class Conduct

Students are expected to manage their time, complete assignments by the due dates, ask questions as needed, and communicate professionally through email, Canvas, phone, or video conference.
