# 📘 Course Syllabus: WKO 101 — Workplace Skills Development I

## Course Overview

This course focuses on foundational workplace skills that support success in professional environments. Students build skills in communication, teamwork, professionalism, time management, and workplace readiness.

**Real-world application:** workplace skills are essential for effective collaboration, productivity, customer service, and career growth in any role.

### Learning Outcomes

- Demonstrate effective workplace communication.
- Apply professionalism and teamwork in a work setting.
- Manage time, tasks, and responsibilities responsibly.
- Develop workplace habits that support productivity and success.
- Prepare for future employment and professional growth.

### Topics Covered

- Workplace professionalism
- Communication and interpersonal skills
- Teamwork and collaboration
- Time management and organization
- Job readiness and workplace expectations
- Career preparation and goal setting

### Assessment

- Participation and reflection activities
- Assignments and check-ins
- Workplace readiness projects
- Skill-building exercises
