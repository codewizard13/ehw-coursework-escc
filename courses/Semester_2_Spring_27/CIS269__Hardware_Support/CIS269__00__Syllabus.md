# 📘 Course Syllabus: CIS 269 — Hardware Support

## Course Overview

This course familiarizes students with computer hardware components, assembly, troubleshooting, and repair procedures. Students learn how computer systems function and how to diagnose common failure points in desktops and related hardware.

**Real-world application:** hardware support skills are essential for IT technicians, field support workers, device repair specialists, and service desk professionals.

### Learning Outcomes

- Identify major computer hardware components and their functions.
- Assemble and configure desktop systems.
- Use troubleshooting procedures to diagnose hardware problems.
- Explain the relationship between hardware, software, and system performance.
- Maintain safe and effective hardware repair practices.

### Topics Covered

- Computer components and architecture
- Motherboards, memory, and storage
- Power supplies and cooling systems
- Peripheral devices and expansion cards
- Diagnostics and troubleshooting
- Maintenance and safety procedures
- System upgrades and repair

### Assessment

- Hardware identification labs
- Troubleshooting simulations
- Practical repair exercises
- Written quizzes and practical checks

### Materials

- Computer components and lab equipment
- Diagnostic tools
- Safety instructions and repair documentation
- Course lab guides
