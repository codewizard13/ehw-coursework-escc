# 📘 Course Syllabus: BUS 180 — Fund of Mgmt Info Systems and the Bus Process

## Course Overview

This course introduces the role of information systems in business operations, decision-making, and management. Students study how technology supports business processes, data management, and organizational strategy.

**Real-world application:** information systems are used across business, operations, finance, and administration to improve efficiency and decision-making.

### Learning Outcomes

- Explain the role of information systems in business operations.
- Describe how data supports managerial decision making.
- Identify common business systems and their functions.
- Evaluate technology-based process improvements.
- Communicate how information systems affect business performance.

### Topics Covered

- Information systems and business processes
- Data, information, and decision support
- Enterprise applications and workflows
- Systems analysis and process improvement
- Management and technology alignment
- Privacy, security, and ethical considerations

### Assessment

- Written assignments
- Business case analysis
- Group activities
- Quizzes and projects

### Materials

- Course text and notes
- Business case studies
- Project documents and templates
- Technology examples and applications
