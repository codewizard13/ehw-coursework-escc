# 📘 Course Syllabus: BUS 296 — Business Internship

## Course Overview

This course provides students with a supervised internship experience in a business or workplace setting. Students apply academic knowledge, develop professional skills, and gain exposure to real work environments and expectations.

**Real-world application:** internships help students build professional networks, strengthen workplace readiness, and transition from classroom learning to career success.

### Learning Outcomes

- Apply classroom concepts in a professional setting.
- Demonstrate professionalism, communication, and teamwork.
- Complete assigned workplace responsibilities and learning goals.
- Reflect on workplace performance and professional growth.
- Connect academic preparation to career readiness.

### Topics Covered

- Internship expectations and professional conduct
- Workplace communication and collaboration
- Task execution and project support
- Time management and accountability
- Reflection and career development

### Assessment

- Worksite performance evaluation
- Supervisor feedback
- Reflective journaling or reports
- Final internship summary or presentation

### Materials

- Internship placement information
- Supervisor evaluation forms
- Course requirements and reflection templates
- Professional guidelines and notes
